# Batch: 17-scanner-adapter-registry

## Purpose
Today every scanner DëvSec runs is described in pieces scattered across five string-keyed dispatch sites — there is no single object that *is* a scanner. Adding (or correcting) a scanner means editing parallel `if scanner == ...` branches in `run_scanner`, `_command`, `_timeout`, the `EXIT_CODES_WITH_FINDINGS` dict, and a separate `normalize()` dispatch, with nothing forcing those facets to stay in sync — easy to wire `run_scanner` but forget `_timeout` or the exit-code set. This batch fixes the single super-list item **S-018**: collapse that scattered scanner knowledge into one adapter registry (a dataclass/protocol entry per scanner carrying its command, timeout, exit-codes, and normalizer), so adding a scanner is one co-located edit. The shared fix surface is the scanner-orchestration seam in `scanners.py` + the `normalize()` dispatch in `normalize.py` (the Rank-3 architecture risk that directly answers the Excellence Brief's "add a scanner without cross-layer surgery" cue).
