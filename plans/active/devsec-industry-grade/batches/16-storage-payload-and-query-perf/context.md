# Batch: 16-storage-payload-and-query-perf

## Purpose
`storage.py` (~3,575 lines) is the repo's densest cross-layer hub: its `dashboard_payload()` method backs `/api/summary` — the dashboard's primary load — yet it does far more than read rows. It embeds live scanner/tool/pack catalogs with install-state detection and runs a per-repo fan-out of ~12–18 small queries for every repo (previous-scan, a 5-query dependency delta, per-scan findings, trust, posture, and resolution runs with a nested per-run items query). This batch owns **S-017** (lift catalog/UI-payload assembly out of `storage.py` so persistence owns schema + queries only, and remove the persistence→scanner-orchestration import inversion) and **S-027** (batch that fan-out into set-based `WHERE scan_id IN (...)` / `run_id IN (...)` queries, killing the per-run `case_resolution_items` N+1). Both act on the one shared fix surface — `storage.dashboard_payload()` and its supporting helpers — so the structural lift (S-017) and the query-batching win (S-027) co-land in a single coherent change.
